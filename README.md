# Whimbrel MedTech Analyst

Save hours of research on US medical device companies. Answers from public records, ranked for your firm, with a source link on every fact.

Your job is to win new business, not to spend hours researching medical device companies. Whimbrel saves you hours of research inside the AI app you already use.

Whimbrel is for sales and business development teams at firms that sell to medical device companies: contract manufacturing, sterilization, testing, design, regulatory and software services.

What you can do:

- Find device companies. Ask about a device space, a technology or a recent event in plain words. Whimbrel finds US device companies that fit and ranks them for what your firm sells.
- Look up one company. See what it makes and what it has done lately, with dates.
- Rank companies you paste. Paste a list of companies or a trade show's exhibitors. Whimbrel puts them in order for your firm and says why each one fits.
- Get the full record. Pull a full research record on one company and send it to HubSpot or Salesforce.

Where answers come from: public records on US medical device companies and their suppliers, including FDA clearances and approvals, recalls, clinical trials, NIH and federal awards, funding filings, company news and hiring. Every fact links to its public source, so you can check it.

Whimbrel learns your firm from its website and from what you tell it. You can see, change or delete what it remembers at any time.

Whimbrel covers US medical device companies only. It does not cover drug companies, hospitals or companies outside the US.

Your first month is free.

## Connect

Add the remote MCP server `https://data.whimbrelresearch.com` (Streamable HTTP) to your AI app. Sign-in uses OAuth; there is no key to paste.
